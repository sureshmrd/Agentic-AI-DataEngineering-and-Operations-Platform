# Metric Definitions

## gross_revenue

Gross revenue is the sum of order item prices.

Source:

monthly_sales_summary.gross_revenue

## total_freight

Total freight is the sum of freight charges.

Source:

monthly_sales_summary.total_freight

## average_order_value

Average order value is calculated as gross revenue divided by the number of
distinct orders.

Source:

monthly_sales_summary.average_order_value

## total_orders

Total orders represents the total number of orders.

Source:

monthly_sales_summary.total_orders

## total_items

Total items represents the total number of order items.

Source:

monthly_sales_summary.total_items

## Data Quality Note
